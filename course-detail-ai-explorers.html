Course Overview

What your child will master in AI Explorers

AI Explorers is VisionX Coders's foundational program for children just starting their tech journey. Students build confidence through block-based coding on Scratch, then take their first steps into Python and AI concepts — all through fun, age-appropriate, hands-on projects. Classes run live, 1-on-1, with a flexible weekday (Mon-Fri, 1hr/day) or weekend (Fri-Sun, 1hr 45min/session) schedule to fit your family's routine.
