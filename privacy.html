VisionX Coders is committed to protecting the privacy of children and their families. We collect only what is necessary and never sell personal data to third parties.

1Information We Collect

When you register or contact us, we may collect:

  • Parent/Guardian Details: Name, email address, and phone number (WhatsApp)
  • Child Details: Child's first name and age group (for class assignment)
  • Communication Data: Messages sent via our contact form or WhatsApp
  • Usage Data: Browser type, pages visited, and session duration (via analytics)

We do not collect payment card details directly. All payments are processed separately and securely.

2How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and manage your child's classes
  • To send class reminders, updates, and course announcements
  • To respond to your enquiries within 24 hours
  • To send our newsletter (only if you subscribed)
  • To improve our website and teaching experience

3Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Google: For Google Meet class sessions and Google Sheets booking records (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Resend: For sending confirmation and notification emails
  • Analytics providers: In aggregated, anonymised form only

4Children's Privacy

VisionX Coders serves children aged 7–14. We take children's privacy very seriously:

  • All registrations must be completed by a parent or guardian (18+)
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children
  • Class sessions occur via Google Meet with a registered adult present or consenting
  • If you believe a child has submitted personal data without parental consent, contact us immediately at visionxcoders@gmail.com

5Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to improve your experience. See our Cookie Policy for full details. You can manage your cookie preferences via the banner shown on your first visit.

6Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including HTTPS encryption and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter via the link in any email

To exercise any of these rights, email visionxcoders@gmail.com.
