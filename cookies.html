1What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, analyse traffic, and deliver a better experience.

2Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies 🔒 (Always On)

These are required for the website to function correctly. They cannot be disabled. They include:

  • visionx-theme — stores your dark/light mode preference
  • vx-cookie-consent — remembers that you have seen the cookie banner
  • vx-cookie-prefs — stores your individual cookie choices

Analytics Cookies 📊 (Optional)

Help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it. Data is anonymised and aggregated — we cannot identify you personally.

Marketing Cookies 📣 (Optional)

Used to show relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. Currently not active — we will notify you when these are enabled.

Functional Cookies ⚙️ (Optional)

Enable enhanced features such as remembering your newsletter subscription status and preferred language settings.

4Managing Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  • Cookie Banner: Click "Manage Preferences" on the banner at the bottom of the page to toggle individual categories on/off
  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via their settings menu
  • Third-party opt-outs: For Google Analytics, visit Google Analytics Opt-out

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.
