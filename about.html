Our Story

Founded by engineers who wanted kids to learn AI for real

VisionX Coders was founded in Islamabad, Pakistan by three passionate partners: Umer Iqbal, Saad Hassan, and Irfan. We saw children growing up surrounded by technology but rarely being taught how to understand or build it themselves.

We built VisionX Coders to bridge that gap — offering live, 1-hour sessions via Google Meet where kids aged 7-14 learn real AI and coding skills in a fun, structured environment with personal attention.

Today we have 75+ students, a team of 5 dedicated educators, and a growing reputation in Islamabad for making AI education genuinely accessible to the next generation of Pakistani innovators.
