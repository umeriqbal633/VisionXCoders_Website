Course Overview

What your child will build in Junior Creator

Junior Creator is a six-month course for children just starting their tech journey. It starts with logic and block coding, moves into Scratch and MakeCode Arcade games, then real Python, micro:bit robotics, and supervised AI tools — finishing with a working AI app your child presents to you live on Demo Day. Classes run five times a week, one hour each, in small groups of no more than five children.

What Your Child Will Learn

Real tools, real projects

🧩

Logic & Block Coding

Blockly, Code.org, and Scratch — ordered instructions, loops, events, and a first debugged program.

🎮

Scratch & Arcade Games

A catcher game with score and collision, a maze with broadcasts, then MakeCode Arcade with enemies and sound.

🐍

Python Foundations

print, variables, input, if/elif/else and loops — ending in a playable text decision game.

🧠

Functions & Data

Functions, lists, dictionaries, and debugging, used to build a quiz engine and Turtle games.

🦾

Robotics & APIs

Files and web APIs, then micro:bit and Wokwi circuits wired and driven from Python.

🤖

AI & Prompt Engineering

Supervised AI tools and prompt engineering, building toward a real working AI app.

🎤

Demo Day Presentation

Learn to explain and present a finished project live to family, out loud, with confidence.

👥

Small-Group Collaboration

Work alongside up to four classmates, discussing approaches and giving feedback every session.
